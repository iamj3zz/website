- type: text
  title: ''
  content: |
    Silt Drift takes as its material the Kowloon district of Hong Kong. A three-dimensional scan of the site is translated into a point cloud, which the artist shapes according to the district's architectural data; this matter responds in real time to sound recordings made on location — the murmur of the bay, but also the sounds of the city: footsteps, cars, pedestrian-crossing signals. From this collection emerges a sensitive, personal translation of Hong Kong, conceived as a tribute to the territory — to its history, its population, its singular blend of cultures — that the artist refuses to see absorbed without remainder. Silt Drift carries this memory at the scale of the dome.
- type: text
  title: Satosphère, Société des Arts Technologiques (SAT) — Montréal (Canada)
  content: |
    A first preview of the piece was presented under the vault of the Satosphère, the 360° immersive dome of the Société des Arts Technologiques in Montréal, of which the artist has been a member since 2026: 360° projection and spatialized sound were tested under real conditions there, a first round of technical challenges the artist is now drawing lessons from.
- type: text
  title: Kunstkraftwerk — Leipzig (Germany)
  content: |
    A second preview, under the same conditions of immersive 360° projection and spatialized sound, was given at Kunstkraftwerk in Leipzig, Germany's first permanent museum entirely dedicated to digital and immersive art. Each site posed its own technical challenges, which the artist chose to confront in order to deepen a practice of the immersive and the augmented real that he intends to carry forward into the French Southern and Antarctic Lands.
